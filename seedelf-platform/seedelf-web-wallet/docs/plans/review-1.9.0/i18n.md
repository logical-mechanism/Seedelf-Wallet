## Findings

### i18n#1 (low, #373, confirmed-by-run): The row says money 'leaves a contract' and the switch asks 'I expect this to leave the contract', but the figure counts money that stays at the same contract

**Files:** seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/en.json:511, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/en.json:513, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/es.json:535, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/ja.json:489, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/wasm/src/cip30.rs:1105, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/ui/screens/DappApprovals.tsx:1108

**Claim.** releasedLovelace and releasedTokens are what doesn't reach the account's own addresses. By design that includes money paid back to the same contract. The row label 'Leaves a contract, not to you' and the switch 'I expect this to leave the contract' say something different: that the money leaves the contract. In an honest repricing or a top-up, which PR #373 itself lists as getting the switch, the window says the listing or vault leaves the contract, and Sign waits for the user to affirm that. Both are false for those flows. Only the callout's 'It doesn't come back to you' matches what is computed. es ('Sale de un contrato', 'Sé que esto sale del contrato') and ja (コントラクトから出ていき…, …出ていくことは想定どおり) carry the same claim.

**Evidence.** cip30.rs:1105-1106: "Money that stays at the same contract counts as gone: a listing can stay where it is under a new owner's datum." The existing test cip30_test.rs paying_the_same_contract_still_counts_as_released says "the money stays there" and asserts released_lovelace 49800000. PR #373's body lists, under "Honest requests that also get the warning and the switch": "Repricing a listing, which pays back to the same contract." architecture.md:563 adds: "an honest listing update needs them."

My test, review_i18n_373_test.rs, run in my worktree with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=$HOME/.cache/seedelf-review-1.9.0/target-i18n cargo test -j 4 --locked -p seedelf-wasm --test review_i18n_373_test -- --nocapture` (6 passed):
- Repricing: the inputs are the account's 5 ₳ and the market's 2 ₳ + NFT with inline datum d8799f581c<key 0/0>ff. The outputs are `out(&market(), 2_000_000, true, Some(&datum))` (the same market, the account's datum) and 4.8 ₳ to ours. required_signers = [key 0/0]. Output: `reprice: net -200000 | contract_inputs 1 holding 2000000 | released 2000000 lovelace + 1 token(s) | key_releases true names_key true -> releases true -> callout contractYours`.
- Top-up: a 1,000 ₳ vault with the account's datum, topped up by 100 ₳, with 1,100 ₳ paid back to the same script. Output: `top-up: net -100200000 | contract_inputs 1 holding 1000000000 | released 1000000000 lovelace`.

Rendering SignTx for the repricing summary (review-i18n-373.test.ts, `npx vitest run tests/review-i18n-373.test.ts --silent=false`):
- en: "Leaves a contract, not to you 2 ₳ Leaves a contract, not to you 1 test", switch "I expect this to leave the contract".
- es: "Sale de un contrato, no hacia ti 2 ₳", "Sé que esto sale del contrato".
- ja: "コントラクトから出ていき、あなたには届かない分 2 ₳", "これがコントラクトから出ていくことは想定どおりです".

DappApprovals.tsx:124 sets `mustAck = … releasesContractFunds(current.summary)`, and the Sign button is disabled while `mustAck && !ack`.

**Scenario.** A user reprices their NFT listing through a marketplace site, so the NFT and 2 ₳ go back to the same contract under their own datum. The window shows "Leaves a contract, not to you 2 ₳" and "… 1 NFT", while Pays lists the same contract receiving both. Sign stays grey until they turn on "I expect this to leave the contract", which isn't what they're doing. Topping up a 1,000 ₳ vault by 100 ₳ shows "Leaves a contract, not to you 1,000 ₳". The user may abandon an honest flow, or learn to flip the switch without reading it.

**Fix.** Label the figure for what it is: the row as "From a contract, not to you", and the switch as "I expect this not to come back to me", which is true of a repricing, a top-up and a re-owned listing alike and still asks a victim the right question. Then redraft es/ja and back-translate them.

### i18n#2 (low, #373, confirmed-by-run): contractYours says 'your own order or listing … It doesn't come back to you' where the code only knows that some datum mentions one of the account's keys

**Files:** seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/en.json:514, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/ui/screens/DappApprovals.tsx:1142, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/wasm/src/cip30.rs:1081, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/wasm/src/cip30.rs:1270, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/shared/rpc.ts:1168

**Claim.** namesKey is true when any 28 bytes of any spent contract UTxO's datum match any of the account's key hashes: payment keys, stake key, or DRep key. It is ORed over every contract input. The code's own comments call the result 'likely' the user's. The stronger callout states three things as fact:
- ownership ('your own'),
- the kind of contract ('order or listing'),
- that all of it is lost ('It doesn't come back to you').
That is false for a Plutus multisig whose datum lists the account among its signers. It is false for a loan repayment, which PR #373 itself names, where the position's ADA and collateral come back. It is false when one contract UTxO names the key and another, which isn't the user's, is the one paid away. The hedged contractMaybe ('helps release', 'If any are yours') is true in every one of these cases. es and ja carry the same claim faithfully.

**Evidence.** cip30.rs:1081-1083: "Says which UTxO is likely the user's, not what the contract does with it". Its check is `b.windows(28).any(|w| … keys.of_hash(&Hash::new(h)).is_some())`, and cip30.rs:1270 ORs it over every input: `names_key |= datum_names_key(row, &carried, &keys);`. rpc.ts:1168: "it's likely the user's own order or listing". architecture.md:563 says "a contract UTxO may be someone else's, with the key only co-signing" and "a loan's repayment can read as releasing the ADA paid on". DappApprovals.tsx:1142: `tr(s.namesKey ? "dappUi.warn.contractYours" : "dappUi.warn.contractMaybe")`.

My run (review_i18n_373_test.rs, 6 passed):
- Treasury: datum `d8799f9f581c<their key>581c<key 0/0>ffff`, required signers both, 999.8 ₳ paid to a vendor. Output: `treasury: net 0 | … released 999800000 … names_key true -> … callout contractYours`. The same transaction with only their key in the datum gives `callout contractMaybe`.
- Loan repaid: a 2 ₳ + token position with the account's datum. The account repays 100 ₳ to a pool and gets 2 ₳ + the token back to ours(Receive,0). Output: `loan repaid: net -98200000 | … released 2000000 lovelace + 0 token(s) | … contractYours`.
- Own order refunded beside another's paid on: the user's 50 ₳ order comes back, and someone else's 30 ₳ contract UTxO goes to a third party. Output: `net 49800000 | contract_inputs 2 … released 30000000 … contractYours`.

Rendering SignTx for the treasury in en: "Your signature releases your own order or listing from a contract. It doesn't come back to you." with "Leaves a contract, not to you 999.8 ₳".

**Scenario.** A DAO signer co-signs a Plutus treasury payout through the DAO's site and is told it releases "your own order or listing". A borrower repaying a loan sees the collateral token arrive in the window's Gets row. The callout above tells them "Your signature releases your own order or listing from a contract. It doesn't come back to you." over a 2 ₳ row, when that 2 ₳ came back with the collateral.

**Fix.** Use only what's computed. Either keep contractMaybe's hedges for namesKey too, or reword contractYours to something like "Funds from a contract that names your key don't come back to you." Then redraft es/ja and back-translate them.

### i18n#3 (low, #373, traced): The Spanish switch says 'I know this leaves the contract', not 'I expect'

**Files:** seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/es.json:535, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/ja.json:487, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/verified-critical-es.json:2

**Claim.** en.json:511 reads 'I expect this to leave the contract': it asks whether the user meant this, and it's what Sign waits for. es.json:535 reads 'Sé que esto sale del contrato', which back-translates as 'I know that this leaves the contract'. That only acknowledges what the callout above just said, and a victim who has read the callout does know it. ja.json:487 'これがコントラクトから出ていくことは想定どおりです' ('…is as I anticipated') keeps the expectation. The verified record says round two 'read both right'.

**Evidence.** verified-critical-es.json, reviewer text: "Round one read \"Espero\" as \"I hope\" … so the switch became \"Sé que esto sale del contrato\" … round two read both right." I rendered it with i18next (scripts/render-373.mjs): es `dappUi.warn.contractAck: Sé que esto sale del contrato`, ja `これがコントラクトから出ていくことは想定どおりです`.

**Scenario.** A site takes the user's DEX order. The Spanish window warns "Tu firma libera de un contrato tu propia orden … No vuelve a ti", then asks for "Sé que esto sale del contrato". That is true the moment they've read the warning, so they flip it. The English asks whether they expected this, which they didn't.

**Fix.** Use "Cuento con que esto salga del contrato" ('I'm counting on this leaving the contract'), with no 'hope' reading, and back-translate it. Or redraft it together with i18n#1's new switch wording.

### i18n#4 (low, #373, traced): The contract-UTxO notes state money and are the only signal in three known limits, but no back-translation check guards them

**Files:** seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/i18n/translations/en.json:430, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/ui/screens/DappApprovals.tsx:1074, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/es-provenance.json:442, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/ja-provenance.json:404, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/critical-keys.json:84

**Claim.** dappUi.note.contractInputs* and contractInputsTokens* say 'It spends N contract UTxOs holding X ₳'. architecture.md:563 says 'That note is all there is' for three cases: a position released by a token, a native script's input in a transaction that also runs Plutus, and an owner-input contract whose datum is kept by hash and withheld. The notes are pushed into a plain note list, so neither the JSX nor the key name makes them critical. Their es/ja values are 'machine' and unverified, so a later edit ships without a back-translation. Today's values are accurate.

**Evidence.** DappApprovals.tsx:1074 `tr(s.contractTokens?.length ? "dappUi.note.contractInputsTokens" : "dappUi.note.contractInputs", …)` is pushed into `notes` and rendered at :1289 as `<ul className="dapp-points note">`. critical-keys.json has no dappUi.note.* key. es-provenance.json:442-447 and ja-provenance.json:404-405 are all "machine". scripts/i18n-critical.mjs's header gives the criterion: "strings where a wrong translation costs a user money or privacy … the set that has to be checked by a person". It names `.warn.` in a key as the way to bring in strings outside callouts (e.g. dappUi.whose.warn.*).

My back-translation of today's values:
- es: "Gasta 1 UTxO de contrato que contiene 1,234.5 ₳." = "It spends 1 contract UTxO that holds…"
- ja: "コントラクトの UTxO を 2 件使用します。保持額は 1,234.5 ₳ です。" = "It uses 2 contract UTxOs. The amount held is…"
Both are right.

**Scenario.** Suppose a later edit drifts the Spanish to 'Lee …' (reads) or drops 'Gasta'. A Spanish user signing a transaction that releases a 500 ₳ position held by their token no longer sees that a contract's 500 ₳ is being spent, and CI passes.

**Fix.** Rename the keys to dappUi.note.warn.contractInputs*, run `node scripts/i18n-critical.mjs --write`, and list them in verified-critical-es/ja. The current values already back-translate correctly.

### i18n#5 (nit, #373, confirmed-by-run): Plain space instead of the house's no-break space before ₳ in the new notes and the released row

**Claim.** glossary.md:151 makes U+00A0 before ₳ the house rule in every language. 1.6.0's review fixed 15 that broke it. #373's four en, six es and two ja note values use a plain space. So does the released row at DappApprovals.tsx:1109 (`${formatAda(s.releasedLovelace!)} ₳`), while the rows beside it use   (lines 1096, 1129, 1132, 1158).

**Evidence.** A python3 -I check of the characters before ₳: every new en/es/ja contractInputs* value has a PLAIN SPACE, while tx.privacy.collateralHint has NBSP. DappApprovals.tsx:1096 has ` ₳`, but :1109 has `} ₳`.

**Scenario.** In the 400 px window, "49.8 ₳" in the released row or the note can wrap between the number and ₳.

**Fix.** Use   in the template at line 1109, and U+00A0 in the twelve note values.

### i18n#6 (nit, #373, traced): The verified-critical record for #373 names no keys, and its 'contract warnings' leaves out tx.privacy.inputsHintSite

**Files:** seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/verified-critical-es.json:2, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/verified-critical-ja.json:2, seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/docs/i18n/es-provenance.json:528

**Claim.** Earlier rounds in the reviewer text name each key and frame they checked, for example "1.8.1 (chunk 43 …): lj.warn.rewardsMoved …". The #373 entry says only "The sign window's contract warnings … were back-translated". tx.privacy.inputsHintSite is a privacy hint in Transaction details, not a contract warning, so nothing records that it was back-translated. PR #373's body does say five strings were. Also, es dappUi.warn.contractAck was corrected after the back-translation, which glossary.md:169 labels mtpe, but it is recorded as machine. That follows house practice: 352 of es's 560 verified critical values carry machine.

**Evidence.** The text the reviewer line gained (diffed with python): "The sign window's contract warnings (a bounty report against 1.8.1, 2026-11-08) were back-translated blind in two rounds. …" and, for ja, "… back-translated blind: every clause held, and nothing needed correcting." Neither names a key. Labels among critical values (python count): es is machine+verified 352, mtpe 204, exact-reuse 4.

**Scenario.** A later reader of the record can't tell whether the inputs privacy hint was ever back-translated, or which values round one changed.

**Fix.** Name the five keys in the entry, inputsHintSite included, and the value round one changed.

## What holds

- Plural forms match Intl.PluralRules in Node 24: en one/other, es one/many/other, ja other. Both new plural keys have exactly those forms, no extras. Rendered with i18next at counts 1, 2 and 1,000,000, es 1,000,000 resolves to _many.
- Placeholders are the same in every language: {{count}} and {{ada}} in each contractInputs* form; the other new keys have none. The i18n.test.ts token check passes.
- My back-translations (not blind: I had read en first) of es/ja contractMaybe, contractRow, contractYours, inputsHintSite, mustBeSignedByYou and the notes match en clause for clause. The negations hold ('Si alguno es tuyo, no vuelve a ti'; 'その中にあなたのものがあれば、あなたには戻ってきません'). The only drift is es contractAck (i18n#3).
- In the bounty's own case (the account's key as required signer, the order paid elsewhere), the callout, row and switch are true in all three languages. Without the datum, contractMaybe's hedged words are true too.
- The warnings never understate what is computed. Each mismatch found errs toward alarm, not toward hiding money.
- tx.privacy.inputsHintSite is true. The worker's resolve() (dapp.ts:1821) looks up the inputs and collateral it doesn't hold through koios(network).utxoInfo, and `site` is passed only from DappApprovals.tsx:1321. #373 also fixed the earlier false 'the wallet asks nobody' in the sign window. That needs no privacy-policy entry, since no new request was added.
- tx.mustBeSignedByYou is true of yourSigners in tx-view.ts markYours: payment and stake keys of the active and other accounts and of sessions. It leaves out the DRep key, which is a harmless under-claim in the details view.
- The five new .warn./.privacy. keys (dappUi.warn.contractAck, contractMaybe, contractRow, contractYours, tx.privacy.inputsHintSite) are in critical-keys.json, which matches the derived set, and in both verified-critical key lists. Provenance is recorded for every new key, using the existing labels.
- Guards pass: 175/175 across nine i18n test files, including the derived critical set, the unchecked-draft gate, provenance per key, plural forms, tokens, negations and the Seedelf name. dapp-prompt, tx-view-screen and tx-view pass 82/82. Together they cover every new key's structure.
- No module-level t(): every new string goes through tr() inside a component (DappApprovals.tsx, TxDetail.tsx). releasesContractFunds in dapp.ts uses no strings.
- The Seedelf name doesn't appear in any new string. All new strings are one or two short sentences, so the short-text rule holds.
- Locale files aren't kept sorted anywhere (180 adjacent pairs out of order in en), so the new keys' placement is not a problem.
- Only #373 touched the locale and docs/i18n files in 1.9.0. #375 and #377 change worker code with no new user-facing strings.

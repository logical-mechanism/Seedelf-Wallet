# PR #337 review (label `pr337`): *Bring the rest back now*

Merge `11e249d2`, item 6 of 1.7.0's list (323-worker#3). Reviewed on HEAD `6bca8bec`.

## Verdict

The change does what it says, and it is safe with money. On a page, an hour after the collateral's deposit landed, a session held at its collateral's mix offers **Bring the rest back now**. Pressing it marks the tail over (`tailOver`). It stops a chain still being sent at the mix (`witnessTail` → `tailIsOver` → `lj.tail.skipped`). Then the next return is a plain one. I found no loss or lock of funds, no new data sent anywhere, and no stuck state.

- **Swaps and mixes that run themselves:** the press brings the rest back directly, in the same request (scratch R5).
- **Locks:** the hold survives a lock, and the way out still works afterwards (scratch R3).

The defects are all low or nit, and they sit at the edges:
- the offer shows where nothing waits for giveme.my;
- by hand, the button brings nothing back, and the stop reason says it did;
- the lock-cut branch and the path for sessions that run themselves have no test;
- a press can be lost against the runner building the mix again;
- small docs and UI gaps.

## Answers to the brief's questions

- **Where the deposit time comes from:** `Tail.landed`. `tailLanded` sets it the first time `witnessTail` runs (every transaction before the mix is on chain by then), or the first time `tailChain` finds the box listed after a cut. It lives in the sealed `lovejoin.<network>` schedule.
  - **A lock:** it survives. The chain is cut, and `tailBuildable` keeps the hold until the day's end; a mix built again carries `landed` over. Scratch R3 shows the offer and the skip working after a lock.
  - **A worker restart:** it survives; nothing it needs is held in memory.
  - **A restore:** there are no chain records on the restored device, so there is no hold and no button. A found session's return there carries no tail, by design ("from the device alone").
- **The race with giveme.my signing:**
  - **Witness already in flight:** if `witnessTail` was past its `tailIsOver` check when the press marked the tail over, giveme.my's signature sends the mix and the chain's own return. The records end up `over` + `scheduled` (`over` is documented as "it went, or won't"). `chainSent` still draws the leaf's due time. The mix's inputs go into the spent set when it is sent and then leave the listing, so *Mix my boxes again* cannot pick the moved box.
  - **Witnessed but not yet sent:** this happens when a worker stop cuts the send. The skip's own pump sends that mix, because `pumpChain` skips `witness` for a witnessed transaction. The outcome is the same, more private than the line promises, and harmless.
  - **"The mix lands *and* the rest comes back directly":** this only happens on a lock between the mix's submit and `chainSent`. That case existed before this PR and behaves the same at the day's end.
- **Pressing twice, or after the day's end:** both are no-ops. `tailHeld` filters out tails that are over, and past `TAIL_KEEP_MS` `tailBuildable` fails. The response is only `advance(…, true)`.
- **A lock mid-skip:** `tailOver` is written before the lock, or the request fails with WalletLocked and nothing changes. A lock during the pump leaves the chain cut and the tail over, so the next return is plain and the reason shown is the cut's. The state stays consistent either way.
- **Each kind of session:**
  - **A swap or a mix that runs itself:** the direct return goes in the same request (R5, a mix session; a swap takes the same `act` branch, `kinds.has("deposit")`).
  - **By hand (a site's session, a swap brought back by hand):** the chain stops, and the rest waits for *Bring it back* (finding 2).
  - **The mix's card:** wired. A swap's or a site's chain entry on the Lovejoin page has no button; their own pages do.
- **Whether the button shows only when the hold is real:** not always (finding 1).
- **Whether the line is literally true:** its privacy clauses are true (unmixed; tied to the session by the deposit's input). The first sentence, "still waiting for giveme.my", is not true in the states of finding 1. "Until you mix it again" is conservative, since someone else's mix may also move the box. In the signing race the box may get mixed after all, which is more private than the line says.
- **The stop reason's words:** they show on the Lovejoin page (the Chains row, and the mix card's detail). On swap and site pages only `chainText` shows. They are false for sessions brought back by hand (finding 2).
- **Tests:** the PR's test does fail without the `witnessTail` stop (my M1 confirms it). It also fails without the skip's pump (M3). The gaps are in finding 3.
- **i18n:** the critical key is recorded in `critical-keys.json` and in both `verified-critical-*` files, and the two drafts are in provenance. `i18n.test.ts`, `i18n-worker-reasons`, `i18n-critical-helpers`, `words`, `worker-words` and `collateral-held` pass when run alone: 141 tests.
- **Privacy policy:** nothing new is sent and no data handling changes. The policy already says "If the mix can't go, the box waits there, and can be tied to the session". No policy entry is needed (`docs/privacy.md` is in finding 5).

## Findings

### 1. (low) The offer and its line show where nothing waits for giveme.my

`tailHeld`'s `tailBuildable` branch (`lovejoin.ts:4131`) holds for a day from `landed`. Nothing marks the tail over when the session's rest comes back directly some other way. `buildBack` skips `tailChain` for a direct return (`sessions.ts:4386`, `if (started && !direct && lovejoin)`), and `restBack` leaves alone a chain whose tail box is out (`restOnly` → `tailBoxOut`).

**Scenario.** A site's session. Its chain stops at the collateral's mix (giveme.my refused past its wait, or a lock cut it). The user then brings everything back with *Bring everything back*'s switch set to *directly*: `session-claim-build` with `direct`, the switch shown when another pick has boxes (`ClaimAll.tsx:286`). The deposit's change is swept, so nothing can pay for that mix any more.

Scratch R1, which called `backBuild(…, direct=true)` and then `backSubmit`, shows the view keeping `collateralHeld: {offerAt}`:
- at stage `returning`;
- still after the return landed, at stage `open`, because a site's session stays open until Disconnect.

From the hour until the day's end, the site's page (`SiteSessions.tsx:410-420`, rendered unconditionally) then shows "The collateral's mix is still waiting for giveme.my. Bring the rest back without it…" and **Bring the rest back now**. The rest is already back, and pressing it only marks the tail over.

A by-hand swap shows the same while its return is `returning`. The line's first sentence is also untrue in two other held states:
- while the mix waits for the pool rather than giveme.my (`lj.tail.fewBoxes` / `lj.tail.noPool`, which a self-running session's retry line shows on the same page);
- while a by-hand session's cut chain waits for *Bring it back*.

**Fix.** The smallest: drop the hold from the view once a return of the rest was sent after the chain. That is exactly `chainView(...).cut` (`sessions.ts:6166`). In `view()`, use `...(stage !== "closed" && tail && !(s.chain && chainView(s.chain, txs).cut) ? { collateralHeld: tail } : {})`. Alternatively, mark the tail over where a plain return of a started session is sent.

Wording the first sentence without giveme.my is the owner's call. It is a critical key, so a new value needs a blind back-translation.

### 2. (low) By hand, *Bring the rest back now* brings nothing back, and the reason says it did

`skipCollateralMix` ends with `advance(network, index, true)` (`sessions.ts:5537`), and `step` returns at once for a session that doesn't run itself (`sessions.ts:3446`, `if (!s || !running(s)) return;`).

**Scenario.** A site's session (or a swap brought back by hand) whose chain waits at the collateral's mix, past the hour. The user presses **Bring the rest back now**. Scratch R2: nothing is submitted (3 → 3 transactions), and the page shows the chain "Stopped after 3 of 5 transactions; the rest stays at the account until you bring it back". The Lovejoin page's Chains row says "Why it stopped: You brought the rest back without waiting for the collateral's mix, so the chain stopped there." The rest is still at the account. It comes back only after *Bring it back*, its review, and Send.

`flows.md:522` says the rest "comes back directly". This holds for sessions that run themselves only.

**Fix.** On the by-hand pages (`SiteSessions.tsx` onSkip, `Swaps.tsx` by-hand branch), open the return's review after the skip: `buildBack(true)`, which then builds a plain return. Also reword `lj.tail.skipped` without the past tense, for example "The rest comes back without the collateral's mix, so the chain stopped there." It is a machine draft, so no back-translation is needed.

### 3. (low) No test for the lock-cut branch or for sessions that run themselves

**Mutation M2**, run in a copy: remove `|| tailBuildable(c, this.deps.now())` from `tailHeld` (`lovejoin.ts:4131`). All 41 collateral tests in `lovejoin-planner.test.ts` and `collateral-held.test.ts` still pass. The cut or stopped chain, the usual way a held chain ends (a lock), is unguarded.

The path for sessions that run themselves is untested too: the skip, then `advance`, then the direct return in the same request (swap pages and the mix card). Scratch R5 shows it works on HEAD: one transaction sent, spending the deposit's change; giveme.my not asked again; `collateralHeld` cleared.

The page wiring (`Swaps.tsx:2513/2567`, `SiteSessions.tsx:411`, `Lovejoin.tsx:2586`) has no test either.

**Fix.** Add a test where a lock cuts the chain while its mix waits (`session.remove(SESSION_CHAIN_PREFIX…)` and `lj.cuts`). Assert that the view still offers the way out from the hour, and that the next return has no `collateralTail`. Add a second test for a mix session that runs itself: after the skip, exactly one direct return is submitted.

### 4. (low) A press is lost against the runner building that mix again

`skipCollateralMix` calls `tailHeld` and then `tailOver` outside the sessions' queue (`sessions.ts:5527-5529`). `recordChain` hands a tail on only `if (was && !was.over)` (`lovejoin.ts:4998-5002`), and the new chain's tail is recorded from `tailOf`'s copy, with no `over` (`lovejoin.ts:4956-4968`).

**Scenario.** A mix or swap that runs itself, whose chain a lock cut while its mix waited. Past the hour, the runner's step (alarm, page advance, or unlock) is in `tailChain` building the mix again. Meanwhile the user presses **Bring the rest back now**.

Scratch R6 drove it through the real `skipCollateralMix` from inside the rebuild:
- chain A's tail is over;
- chain B is recorded with its tail not over (`tries: 2`);
- giveme.my is asked again (asked 1 → 2);
- the press's own response still carries `collateralHeld`, because B carries A's `landed`.

The user has to press again. A review built before the press and sent after it does the same (scratch R4).

**Fix.** Run the press inside `this.serial`: then `tailHeld` sees B, and marks it over. Or have `recordChain` start the new chain's tail `over` when the tail it takes over was already ended.

### 5. (nit) Docs

- `docs/privacy.md:217`, the design doc for this ground, still has the box wait unmixed only "past that, or a day after the deposit landed". It doesn't mention the user's way out from an hour.
- `post-release-roadmap.md:40` still marks item 6 ⬜, as it does items 5 and 7 (maybe left to the release PR).

### 6. (nit) Small UI gaps

- The mix card renders its wrapper `<div className="token-row__detail">` (`Lovejoin.tsx:2586-2590`) during the hour before the offer, even though the component inside returns null: an empty grid row with a 4 px margin.
- The site's page and a by-hand swap's page don't poll. The button appears at `offerAt` only after the next Refresh or re-render (`now = Date.now()` is read at render).

### 7. (nit, low confidence) No way out where the deposit's landing was never recorded

`tailChain` returns `"noPool"` before it records `landed` (`lovejoin.ts:4040-4041` against `4056`/`4063`), and `tailHeld` requires `landed` (`4130`).

**Scenario.** A chain is cut while its collateral's deposit is in flight. The 5 ₳ pool then drops out of the read set, which needs `known()` to fail after a worker restart. The return then waits up to `TAIL_KEEP_MS` from the chain's end with no offer. This looks very rare.

# pr333: PR #333, a private Bootstrap's way back priced from the parameters

Merge `b2c3940f` (`git diff b2c3940f^1 b2c3940f`). Reviewed on HEAD `6bca8bec`. No later PR touches #333's lines: #335 merged 44 s before it (its merge is #333's first parent), and #336 and #337 change sessions.ts, lovejoin.ts and Lovejoin.tsx elsewhere.

## Verdict

**The money side is right.** `bootstrap_back_reserve` is an upper bound on what the way back costs, for these reasons:

- The Seedelf output's least is exact.
- The sweep's fee is taken as drafted: `max(linear_fee(400), FIRST_DRAFT_FEE)`, and `settle`'s first draft is 0.2 ₳.
- The deposit's fee is bounded by `linear_fee(MAX_TX_SIZE)`. `settle` refuses a draft past it, and a deposit runs no script and reads no reference script.
- It rounds up to 0.1 ₳, with a floor of 1.5 ₳.

The funding and the cap use one parameter read (`mixOutBuild`), and the form's bounds use another single read (`privateBootstrapBounds`). Every caller of the new `lovejoinBootstrapReserve(params)` passes parameters. The pkg's `.d.ts` has the new signature, and no zero-argument caller is left in `src/`, `tests/` or `e2e/`.

**The real chain builds at the cap at any price** (scratch run, below). Funded exactly as the worker funds it, the real chain builder (`chain_in`: the deposit, then the sweep back) goes at `bootstrap_most`'s cap:

- in all 14 parameter rows: 4,310 to 20,000 lovelace a byte, `min_fee_a` 44 to 1,000, `min_fee_b` up to 2 ₳;
- in the classic pool and two token pools.

Once the reserve is priced above its floor, it leaves 0.1 to 0.2 ₳ over at the cap.

**What's wrong is small: the form, the tests and the docs.**

- The form still counts a fixed 2 ₳ `FEE_ROOM` beside the priced reserve. Above about 5,200 lovelace a byte it passes a balance the funding refuses, and the PR's own form test asserts such a balance as enough.
- The page's wiring of `back` has no test.
- flows.md and architecture.md still say a dearer byte lowers the cap.
- Core's dear-byte token check uses a pool whose box is below the ledger's minimum at that price.

Nothing high or medium.

## The legacy check (the brief's question)

**A Bootstrap that 1.6.0 funded can't be taken for a legacy one.** At `babb8e76`, sessions.ts:2171 already writes `...(built.backReserved ? { noCollateral: true as const } : {})`. `back` was always 1.5 ₳ for a Bootstrap, so every private Bootstrap 1.6.0 funded is marked. HEAD's legacy branch requires `!record.mix.noCollateral` (sessions.ts:4451), and the collateral pick returns `undefined` for a marked record (sessions.ts:4428-4430).

- Only `mixOutSubmit` writes a session's `mix.bootstrap`.
- The only unmarked records are from `62162483`..`fc8561c7`. Both commits are after 1.5.0 (`63f87b8`) and inside 1.6.0, so dev builds only, as the comment says.
- Those records paid no way-back reserve, so `paid < boxesOnly + reserve` holds for any reserve of 1.5 ₳ or more. Using today's figure changes nothing for them.

What happens at a dearer byte, run through the worker with the real WebAssembly (scratch test, 96 boxes funded at 4,310 with 1.5 ₳ back, the chain built at 6,465):

| Case | What happened |
|---|---|
| **B:** marked, a stranger's 5 ₳ of ADA alone at the account | Not put up as collateral (the chain got `collateral: none`). The deposit spent it beside the funding, as it spends every ADA-only UTxO at the account, and its ADA covered the shortfall: all 96 boxes went in, then the sweep. |
| **B2:** marked, nothing else at the account | The chain fails: `the wallet couldn't build its chain: a Seedelf deposit of these tokens needs at least 2.185170 ADA`. The session comes back directly: `out`, `back`, no box in, and a second fee (963 ₳ in, 962.829439 ₳ swept back). See finding 5. |
| **C:** the same record unmarked | The legacy branch goes in with 95 boxes, and the 96th box's ADA pays the way back. |

So the return never takes a stranger's 5 ₳ as collateral. A 1.6.0 funding whose chain is built after a price rise fails its chain and comes back directly. That was already the case before #333, and still is for a 1.7.0 funding priced at today's parameters.

## Findings

### 1. low (money/ux): the form's sum still has a fixed 2 ₳ fee room, so it doesn't match the funding "at any price"

- **Files:**
  - `extension/src/ui/screens/Lovejoin.tsx:3662-3663`;
  - `Lovejoin.tsx:207-211` (`FEE_ROOM`: "a Seedelf output's, 1.46 ₳ at today's 4,310 lovelace a byte, with room for a dearer byte");
  - `extension/tests/lovejoin-release-rules.test.ts:503-506`.
- **Claim:** the PR says the form's figure "matches what the funding takes, at any price". Only the reserves follow the price: `costs = putting × box + (1.5 ₳ + back) + FEE_ROOM`. `FEE_ROOM` holds the funding's fee (about 0.23 ₳) and its change's Seedelf least (1.457 ₳ at 4,310). At 6,465 the change's least alone is 2.185 ₳. From about 5,200 lovelace a byte up, the form calls a balance enough that the funding refuses.
- **Scenario:** an empty wallet with one private UTxO of exactly 305.3 ₳, at 6,465 lovelace a byte, asks to Bootstrap 30 classic boxes.
  - The form: `bootstrapLimits(…, back "1800000")` says not short, and the PR's test asserts exactly this (`ada(needs + 300_000n)` with `.short` false at line 506).
  - The worker: `mixOutBuild("preprod", 30, { bootstrap: true })` refuses, "Not enough ADA in your private balance for this, its fee and the change."
  - 305.7 ₳ is refused too, and 306.0 ₳ funds (fee 0.230260 ₳, change 2.469740 ₳ against a least of 2.185170 ₳).
  - At 4,310, 305 ₳ (the form's figure) funds.
  - The money is safe and the Review refusal is in words. This is the form-passes, Review-refuses gap that MONEY-6 closed, back at a dearer byte.
- **Fix:** have `privateBootstrapBounds` also return the funding's change least under the same parameters, and use that (plus a fee allowance) in `FEE_ROOM`'s place once known. The WebAssembly already computes it as the funding summary's `changeMinimum`; a small export over `minimum_seedelf_payment` would do too. Correct line 506's expectation: 305.3 ₳ is short at 6,465, 306 ₳ isn't. The Mix stepper shares `FEE_ROOM`, but that's older and outside this PR. If the fixed room stays, the minimal fix is to correct the test and the claim.
- **Test:** at 6,465 with exactly 305.3 ₳ private, `mixOutBuild(30, bootstrap)` is refused while `bootstrapLimits(..., bounds.back)` says not short. It fails today and passes once the form counts the priced change least.
- **Confidence:** high (measured).

### 5. owner-call (low): a marked Bootstrap whose chain is built after the byte got dearer comes back directly with a second fee

My idea, not a defect of #333.

- **Files:** `extension/src/background/sessions.ts:4445-4454` (the one-box-fewer rule, for unmarked records only) and `sessions.ts:4479-4486`.
- **Claim:** the reserve is priced at the review. The chain is built when the funding lands, or after the next unlock if the wallet locked first. Once priced above the floor, the reserve leaves only 0.1 to 0.2 ₳ over at the cap: measured 0.115 ₳ at 6,465 and 0.097 ₳ at 8,000. At today's price the floor leaves 0.54 ₳.
  - A parameter rise in between fails the sweep. The session then comes back directly, with no box in, a second fee, and core's raw message as the reason, which says "of these tokens" for an output with none.
  - The legacy rule (one box fewer, its ADA paying the way back) would carry it in, as case C shows, but it's limited to unmarked records.
- **Scenario:** 96 classic boxes are funded at 4,310 (1.5 ₳ back) and the wallet locks before the funding lands. The byte goes to 6,465 at an epoch boundary, and at the next unlock the chain fails (case B2).
- **Proposal:**
  - apply the same rule to a marked Bootstrap whose paid funding is short of `lovejoin.funding(boxes) + lovejoinBootstrapReserve(params)` under the chain's parameters (fewer boxes, the rest paying the way back);
  - or keep coming back directly, and give it words of its own rather than core's.
- **Confidence:** medium on how much it matters: it needs a protocol-parameter rise inside that window.

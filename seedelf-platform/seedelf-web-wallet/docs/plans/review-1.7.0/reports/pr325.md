## Findings

### 1. low (docs): "Done that way" leaves out two steps its own plan names

- **Evidence:** `roadmap.md:62` plans "the API's deploy from the merge commit (Actions → *Data layer deploy*, `deploy`, then `status`) … then the package on the merge commit, e2e on it, checklist item 9, the upload …, and the record PR". `roadmap.md:63` answers "**Done that way:**" and records the deploy, the curl checks, the package and e2e. It says nothing of `status` or of item 9.
  - `gh run list --workflow data-layer-deploy.yml` shows no `status` run on `babb8e76`: 40440233291 (deploy) is followed only by 40713719454, a deploy two days later.
  - The packaging session's transcript hands item 9 off: "The owner does checklist item 9 locally". No outcome was recorded anywhere.
  - `development.md:205`/`:280` ask for the `status` run, and `:244`/`:288` for item 9 by hand on the store build.
- **Scenario:** 1.7.0's release, or anyone auditing 1.6.0, reads the record to learn whether the shipped build was checked by hand on mainnet. That is item 9, the only check of the store build against the real data layer and its host list (the worker's network panel). "Done that way" reads as yes. If item 9 was skipped, a mainnet-only fault in the shipped build went unchecked while the record says the plan was followed. The next record, copied from this one, can drop item 9 silently.
  - The missing `status` run is harmless. The deploy printed `DEPLOYED 942e3bbc… (3b03a0e… kept as .prev)`, which is what `status` shows (`seedelf-data/deploy/README.md:146`).
- **Fix:** add one clause to the record: "checklist item 9 by hand, the owner's: <outcome>" (or "not run"), and "the deploy's own `DEPLOYED` line stood in for `status`".
  - Separately, an owner's call: since the deploy prints what `status` does, either drop "then a `status` run" from `development.md:205`/`:280` and the runbook's "after its `status` run", or keep running it.
- **Confidence:** high that the record omits both. Whether item 9 was done is unknown.

### 2. nit (release): the C compiler line is paraphrased, without its package revision

- **Evidence:** `development.md:248` and `:291` keep `wasm/build.sh`'s last line (`build.sh:77`). The line printed was `C compiler: Ubuntu clang version 21.1.8 (6ubuntu1)` (`owner-package-1.6.0.log:283`), and the record has "Ubuntu clang 21.1.8".
- **Scenario:** someone rebuilds 1.6.0 to check the store's zip with another 21.1.8 build. The record can't say whether their compiler is the one that built it. The dropped `(6ubuntu1)` is the part apt changes under a fixed upstream version: the same 2026-10-13 upgrade moved `clang-18` from `18.1.3-1ubuntu1` to `18.1.8-20ubuntu8`. 1.5.0's record shortened its line the same way.
- **Fix:** copy the line as printed.
- **Confidence:** high on the fact, low on the harm.

# PR #334: review (label `pr334`)

**PR:** #334, merge `ae610284`, "docs(web-wallet): 1.7.0's list gains a headless Leios run". Docs only: 7 added lines in `docs/post-release-roadmap.md`, item 8 on the list and its plan under *Kept in mind*. #338 (`6bca8bec`) later replaced both with what the run found, so the text on HEAD is #338's.

## Verdict

As a docs change it's fine.
- **No dates, no promises.** Neither #334's text nor #338's dates a wallet release, states the store's status, or promises a wallet change in 1.7.0. The dates in them are of decisions and of the run, as the house style has it. The one external date, #334's "mainnet hard fork is targeted for late 2026", is gone on HEAD.
- **Nothing reached the tree.** `constants.rs`, `references.rs` and `networks.ts` are untouched in the range, and Leios, Musashi and Dijkstra appear nowhere outside the roadmap.
- **Numbering:** the list runs 1 to 8 with no gaps, nothing else counts its items, and item 8 has its **(1.7.0)** entry.

#338's text matches HEAD's code and the run's own records, with two exceptions:
- The chain window is two for four-box mixes, not four.
- "All of it ran" is too broad: no withdraw was ever sent.

**The list's statuses on HEAD:** items 4 to 7 are stale. That is deliberate (#333: "the 1.7.0 list's tick waits for the release-prep docs commit"), but the release-prep commit has nine lines to fix, two of which need rewriting, not just a tick.

**One owner-call:** #338 dropped #334's date for the hard fork. Nothing on HEAD now says the fork is the deadline for the Dijkstra fix.

## Findings

### 1. low · docs: the "window of four" is two for four-box chains (confidence: high)
- **Files:** `docs/post-release-roadmap.md:409`; `extension/src/background/lovejoin.ts:294-302`, `:4635`; `extension/src/background/sessions.ts:4937`; `docs/lovejoin-mix-width.md:149`
- **Claim:** HEAD says the wallet sends a chain four at a time and that sending the whole chain saves "120 to 245 s". A chain of four-box mixes goes two at a time. The 245 s was measured with four in flight, which the wallet never uses for four-box mixes.
- **Scenario:**
  1. Someone takes up the Leios change and raises or drops `CHAIN_WINDOW`.
  2. They leave `CHAIN_WINDOW_FOUR` at 2.
  3. A four-box chain (Settings' four-wide option) still goes two at a time. For a deposit and 5 mixes that's three trips through the queue, not the two the 245 s figure measured.
- **Evidence:**
  - roadmap.md:409: "a chain's window of four in flight (`pumpChain`) could be the whole chain, since four at a time, the rest waited for the queue again (120 to 245 s more)".
  - lovejoin.ts:300-302: `export const CHAIN_WINDOW_FOUR = 2;` … `export const chainWindow = (width: number) => (width >= 4 ? CHAIN_WINDOW_FOUR : CHAIN_WINDOW);`
  - lovejoin.ts:4635: `...(built.width === 4 ? { window: chainWindow(4) } : {}),`
  - sessions.ts:4937: `const window = summary.lovejoin?.width === 4 ? { window: chainWindow(4) } : {};`
  - lovejoin-mix-width.md:149: "`CHAIN_WINDOW`, whose four in flight assume three mixes a block, and `CHAIN_WINDOW_FOUR`, two for four-box mixes (`chainWindow`)".
  - The run's record `origin/web-wallet/leios-musashi:seedelf-leios/results/2026-10-28/1793219148-chain-w4-k4.json`: `"window": 4`. The first four landed at 153.0 s, mixes 4 and 5 at 397.9 and 398.9 s: that gap is the 245 s.
  - The windows predate the range (`CHAIN_WINDOW_FOUR` is from `b9c35f06`, chunk 34e), so the text was wrong when it was written.
- **Fix:** "a chain's window in flight (`pumpChain`: four, two for four-box mixes, `chainWindow`) could be the whole chain: sent four at a time, the rest waited for the queue again (120 s more three wide, 245 s four wide; the wallet sends four wide two at a time, which wasn't run)."

### adapool#0

**Medium · code · reachable · proven by test.** A mix session from the private balance in the 5 ₳ pool leaves its collateral's mix no boxes to draw, while its review promises that mix.

- **Where:** `src/background/lovejoin.ts:3551` (`holdTail` a return's alone), `:3630` (fanOut's room), `:3377` (againBoxes: as many as fit), `:3292-3294` (fits: room for the boxes only); `src/background/sessions.ts:2157` (mixFunding: `collateralMix: reserve ? "mixed" : "ifLeft"`); `src/ui/screens/Lovejoin.tsx:181-183` (reviewHint), `:3853`, `:3934` (CollateralMixNote).
- **Claim:** every ADA mix now goes into the v2 5 ₳ pool, the pool the session's collateral is mixed into. A return's chain leaves that mix its two draws (`roomIn`, fix 8019541f), but a mix session's never does (`holdTail` needs `boxes === undefined`), and neither the tile's Mix (`fits`) nor Mix my boxes again (`againBoxes`) counts those two draws at review. The review still says the collateral is mixed wherever its reserve is paid: "…the 5 ₳ kept aside comes back later, mixed once" and the critical `swaps.review.privacy.collateralMix` ("giveme.my backs its collateral's mix too, so it sees that mix…"). Built, the chain takes the room, WebAssembly finds fewer than two boxes left for the collateral's mix, and the 5 ₳ comes back with the rest, unmixed and tied to the session. Mix my boxes again takes as many boxes as fit by its own rule, so once the wallet's boxes fill the room its collateral is mixed only when the free boxes not the wallet's, mod (mixes a box × 2), are 2 or more: never at depth 1, and 6 pool sizes in 8 at depth 2. Before 1.8.0 a mix went into the classic pool and the collateral into a different 5 ₳ pool, so they never shared room (only a mix in that 5 ₳ pool did).
- **Scenario:** depth 2, the 5 ₳ pool holding 9 boxes not the wallet's and 3 of its own. Mix my boxes again from the private balance: the review has 1 box, a 1.5 ₳ reserve, `collateralMix: "mixed"`, the "mixed once" hint and the giveme.my note. Funded and run, its chain draws 8 of the 9 and has no collateral's mix: the 5 ₳ comes back in the return. At depth 1 with 21 others and 11 of the wallet's boxes: 10 boxes, the same review, no collateral's mix. The tile's Mix of 1 box at depth 2 with 8 or 9 others: the same. With 10 others the collateral's mix goes.
- **Evidence:** `tests/adapool-scratch-mix.test.ts` (sandbox), from `extension/`: `npx vitest run tests/adapool-scratch-mix.test.ts --maxWorkers=1 --silent=false`, 6 passed, printing `[again d2 m3 s9] review renders the giveme.my note: true`, `boxes 1 collateralMix=mixed reserve=1500000 hint=…the 5 ₳ kept aside comes back later, mixed once…`, then `chain boxes 1, tail none`; `[again d1 m11 s21] boxes 10 collateralMix=mixed…`, `chain boxes 10, tail none`; the tile's Mix `[8]` and `[9]` `drew 8 strangers' boxes, chain boxes 1, tail null`; `[10]` and `[again d2 m3 s10]` with a collateral's mix. The same "mixed" promise breaks for a return in roomIn's one-box exception (8 or 9 free two waves deep: its one box goes, the collateral's mix has fewer than two), which a swap's approval says "mixed" for when its reserve is paid.
- **Fix:** count the collateral's two draws where its pool is the mix's own: in `fits` and `againBoxes` (and the Mix form's room), take boxes from `free − TAIL_DRAWS`, and give a mix session's chain `holdTail` too; Mix again then leaves its last box for the next press. Or, keeping the boxes, say `collateralMix: "ifLeft"` (and the plain hint) when the boxes leave fewer than two free.

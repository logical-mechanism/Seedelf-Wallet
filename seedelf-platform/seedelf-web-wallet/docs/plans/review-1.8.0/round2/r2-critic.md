## r2-critic#0: Stopped partway, the first unlock's retired run leaves the boxes it hadn't taken off Home for two hours: critic#3 dropped their count, and Stop moves the read two hours on

**Severity:** low · **Kind:** code · **Introduced:** new · **Lanes:** retire · **Proven:** test

**Where:**
- seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/background/lovejoin.ts:4236
- seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/background/lovejoin.ts:4245

- seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/background/lovejoin.ts:6435
- seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/src/ui/screens/Home.tsx:1494

**Claim:** critic#3's fix drops 1.7's not-mixed counts in the retired pools at retireOnce and sets `recheck` to now, so the pools get read for those boxes (lovejoin.ts:4236-4245). A run that sent something and then stops sets `recheck` to now + SPENT_KEEP_MS over that value. That covers Settings' Stop (:6919) and the run stopping by itself after BRING_FAILED_RUNS with `back > 0` (:7177). Until that read, a box the run hadn't taken has no due time and no count. held() and Home's In Lovejoin leave it out for two hours. At the base, Home kept showing 1.7's count, which over-counted (critic#3's finding). In this state the fix turned that into an under-count. Nothing is stranded: the two-hour read gives the box its wait. Neither r2-screens (Home is in its not-checked list) nor the retire lane's tests reach a Stop after a partial send.

**Scenario:** A 1.7 wallet holds two classic boxes not mixed yet (a cut chain's) and is updated to 1.8.0. At the first unlock the retired run sends one box, giveme.my refuses the other, and the user presses Stop. A user pressing Stop after the first withdraw gets the same. For two hours Home reads 'In Lovejoin · Your mixes', with no box and no 10 ₳, while a 10 ₳ box still sits in the classic pool. Only then does it say '1 box of 10 ₳ · Next back …'. If giveme.my goes down partway, the run stops by itself after three runs that send nothing, and the result is the same.

**Evidence:** Scratch test /tmp/claude-1000/-home-logic-Documents-LogicalMechanism-Seedelf-Wallet/8420b6d7-4a5c-4f2d-934b-2d1b5eec9799/scratchpad/r2/r2-critic/seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/tests/r2-critic-home-after-stop.test.ts uses critic#3's own heldBy17. giveme.my takes the first withdraw and refuses the second, then stopBringingAllBack runs, then a run every 30 min. Command: `npx vitest run tests/r2-critic-home-after-stop.test.ts --maxWorkers=2 --silent=false` gives 1 passed. Its timeline: min 0, 30, 60 and 90 have boxes 0, notMixed 0, due null and Home 'In Lovejoin Your mixes'. Min 120 has boxes 1 and Home 'In Lovejoin 1 box of 10 ₳ 10 ₳ Next back Today, 05:30'. The box is still among the pool's rows throughout. Base: the same test with lovejoin.ts from 1beba3fb (swapped in, then restored and checked by sha256) shows Home 'In Lovejoin 2 boxes of 10 ₳ 20 ₳ Not on their way back 2 not mixed yet' from min 0 to 90. Both builds pass at 4b4628e9.

**Fix:** Read the pools at the next run after such a Stop, and still read them at SPENT_KEEP_MS. Don't let a Stop push critic#3's earlier `recheck` later. Then re-arm `recheck` to the spent hold's end while one of the run's withdraws isn't on chain. The early read gives each box left its wait, and the run's own withdraws stay out of it as spent (split's `unspent`). A plain `Math.min` alone would lose the two-hour read a dropped withdraw needs where no other box is left. Or have held() count a retired pool's boxes from the last pool read while `bringStopped.retired` and no due time covers them. Pin it with this Stop-partway case.

**Claim:** lookSoon's throttle is `scanned`, a Map on the PoolDiscovery instance (lovejoin-pools.ts:277). sw.ts makes that instance once per worker life (:237), and its own comment says 'Worker timers don't survive restarts' (:71). An MV3 worker stops after about 30 s idle, so each new worker starts with no throttle. 'At most every REFRESH_MS a network' therefore holds within one worker's life only. Several places say it with no hedge: the find lane's report, the code's comments (lookSoon :414, adaPool :4159, bringAllBack :6868, sw.ts :235), and the docs writer's drafts (the policy's 6 November entry and its Lovejoin's pools line; privacy.md:202 in the merged worktree, 'at most every 5 minutes a network'). The house already hedges the same in-memory throttle for the data layer's list: the policy at :46 has 'again only after five minutes while the browser keeps the wallet running', and privacy.md:200 has 'while the worker runs: the throttle is in its memory'. o-design noted it too ('The throttle lives in memory … bounded by what the user does'). The request names nothing of the user's. What's overstated is when and how often it comes.

**Scenario:** Preprod, or mainnet with Koios only, with no 5 ₳ pool kept. A swap's Review looks, with one holder read. The user closes the side panel and the worker stops idle. A minute later they reopen it and review a site session's funding. A new worker with an empty `scanned` reads the holder again, 60 s after the first read. Each Settings' Bring every box back pressed after a restart looks too. Koios sees several looks from one IP within five minutes, each at the moment of the user's action.

**Evidence:** Scratch test /tmp/claude-1000/-home-logic-Documents-LogicalMechanism-Seedelf-Wallet/8420b6d7-4a5c-4f2d-934b-2d1b5eec9799/scratchpad/r2/r2-critic/seedelf-platform/seedelf-web-wallet/extension/tests/r2-critic-throttle.test.ts makes a PoolDiscovery as sw.ts does and stands in for a restart with a new instance on the same storage. Command: `npx vitest run tests/r2-critic-throttle.test.ts --maxWorkers=2 --silent=false` gives 3 passed. One instance with asks 60 s apart reads the holder once. A restart between them reads it twice: `R2CRITIC-THROTTLE {"reads":2,"apartMs":60000,"REFRESH_MS":300000}`. Five restarts 50 s apart read it 5 times within REFRESH_MS. Both builds pass.

**Fix:** Say it the way the list's line does: 'at most every five minutes while the browser keeps the wallet running' in the policy's entry and its Lovejoin's pools line, and 'while the worker runs: the throttle is in its memory' in privacy.md. Fix the four code comments the same way. Or keep `scanned` in chrome.storage.session, which is cleared with the browser, so the five minutes hold across restarts and the drafted words stand. Either way, the policy says 'at most' only of what the code bounds.

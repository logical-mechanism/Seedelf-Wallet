### e5#1 · low · Send, Make public and Contacts refuse an address with a key payment part and a script stake part, and every Byron address, in words that misdescribe them

**Scenario:**

Seedelf Wallet pays only ordinary addresses: a payment to a script could be locked there for good." The payment can't be locked, because the key holder spends it and no datum is needed. The ledger accepts the payment. A Byron (base58) address is refused the same way with "That isn't a Cardano address.", though Conway still takes outputs to bootstrap addresses. The user can't pay either kind from the wallet on either side, and the words are false. No money is lost.

## Checked and holds

- Own versus stranger is decided by payment key only (destination.ts ownAccount over WASM is_own_address). base(stranger key, our stake key) is `staked`, never own, so the #359 class holds for Send and Make public. enterprise(our stake hash) read as anyone's is the backlog's own#2.
- The account's UTxOs are read by payment credential only (account.ts:120-123, credential_utxos). A stranger's base(their key, our stake) UTxO never reaches coin selection or the collateral pick.
- Handle lookup: plain name first, then the CIP-68 222 token, never the 100 reference token. The resolved address goes through checkPayable, so a handle sitting at the Seedelf contract or any script payment address is refused.
- A Seedelf recipient: WASM rechecks the UTxO (contract payment credential, holds that name, inline register datum) and is_payable on the register (recipient_register, check_register). TS never hands over a register of its own.
- send.ts, withdraw.ts and transfer.ts zip their summaries with the destinations by index. WASM returns payments in request order (account_send's zip with built.paid, withdraw_result's Amounts), so each label matches its payment.
- Collateral: account_send_many pays recipients in order, then change, so `${txHash}#0` recorded by submitCollateral is the 5 ₳ output, on the account the review was built on.
- Every kept spend through script-spend.ts send runs these checks in order: kept-mismatch with refuseSent first, the 10-minute TTL, settleMaybeSent, refuseSpentSince (private), the signing-hash check, refuseReserved, then the write-ahead submit.
- The account-paid mint's result carries no seed, so Send treats it as signed at review and asks giveme.my nothing.
- writeAhead seals before Koios is asked, and an unsealable payment isn't sent. A busy answer, an unreadable answer, another id, or 'spent' after an earlier try stays maybe sent. A definite refusal takes back exactly what was written ahead and puts back another transaction's spent stamps.
- A public maybe-sent payment is let go only past its slot by the chain's tip (+30 min) and after 2 h of age. A private one is let go after 20 min unseen unless a resend found it in a mempool (≤2.5 h), which matches the backlog's accepted 'no validity interval' item. Home's words for it are hedged ('most likely… check Activity').
- Taken private transactions keep no inputs in the watch, so the unseen and expired paths can't touch them. The resend path re-checks the watch is unchanged before and after Koios is asked, and settles are de-duplicated per transaction.
- Parts: whole() sums per-part lovelace, fees and change. A carry a later part spends is never counted as change (carry_kept), so change isn't double-counted. The review's least appears only when every part sends tokens.
- Coin control: collateral and locked UTxOs are filtered before any account builder sees the UTxOs, and locked private UTxOs before any Seedelf spend, Max included. A Seedelf UTxO and the collateral can't be locked.
- cbor.ts: every head is bounded by the buffer. Depth is counted for lists, maps, tags and indefinite items (a stray 0xff's pseudo-container alternates with counted levels, so the recursion is bounded). txWithdrawals reads coin as bigint. txInputs handles list and tag-258 sets.
- spent.ts and sent-txs.ts: outpoints are kept 2 h by time, not count. Site spends are kept under their own cap. The wallet's own sends and chain steps are capped separately. Sent records are per network, and a refused send is forgotten.
- histories.ts: merged() canonicalizes the legacy 'public'. historiesNote counts by canonical id. carried() gives each carry the merged history of the part before, and unknown money stays out of classesOf.
- Remove: WASM refuses a UTxO not under this wallet's register or not holding exactly one Seedelf. `to` is the active account's 0/0 or back into the contract.
- Recipients 1 to 20 are checked in TS and again in WASM. Labels are printable ASCII up to 15, with WASM check_label too.
- Move-in: submit refuses a missing or stale kept copy through refuseSent, enforces the TTL and settleMaybeSent, and sends the same signed bytes again on Send again.

## Notes

- For E3a: cbor.ts refuses a transaction whose outer array is indefinite-length (`tx[0] !== 0x84`, bodyField and txId) or whose body map is indefinite (bodyField 'bodyNotMap'). Both are well-formed CBOR. As I understand cardano-ledger's decodeRecordNamed/decodeMapLenOrIndef they're accepted, so a site's transaction so encoded can't be signed or submitted. Only Dijkstra's three-element shape is documented (backlog Leios line). Not traced through Pallas's Conway decoder, which may refuse it too.
- For the owner and E1: move-in.ts submit (132-162) calls no refuseReserved, unlike every kept spend through script-spend.ts send. A move-in reviewed before a public Lovejoin mix started being sent can share that chain's first input. The mempool refuses whichever comes second, so nothing is lost, but the refusal is Koios's 'spent' rather than chainConflict's words, and Koios sees the move-in.
- For E3a: dapp.ts keepSent calls rememberSiteSpent whenever Koios answers a site's submitTx busy (5xx, timeout, unreadable). The listed inputs are then held as spent for 2 h, whatever the bytes. If a site can provoke a busy answer, it can hide the public account's UTxOs from balances and builds until a lock. I didn't check whether a site can provoke one.
- For E3a and E4a: isCollateralShaped (coin-control.ts:184) checks only lovelace and tokens. A stranger's 5 ₳ UTxO carrying a reference script or a datum can become the auto-picked collateral. I found no harm: Conway doesn't charge collateral inputs' reference scripts, and the account mint prices only spent inputs. getCollateral hands it to sites as is.
- For the owner: Send's refusal of the user's own Seedelf (send.ts:179, flows.md:231) is documented and points to Make private, which pays an equivalent register. Under the 2026-10-19 rule it's still a refusal of a valid payment. It also stops paying one's own Seedelf in the same transaction as other recipients. Not reported as a finding.
- For E8b: RemoveResult reports only `lovelace`. Other tokens in the Seedelf's UTxO go to `to` with the ADA, which only a stranger-given Seedelf UTxO would hold. I didn't check whether the Remove screen lists them from the balance.
- whole()'s `inputs` include the carries one part makes and the next spends, so the summary's input count is over by the number of carries. No screen shows the whole's count (Transfer/Withdraw reviews checked), so no finding.

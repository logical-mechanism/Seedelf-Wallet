# e8a#3: verification

From lane E8a, graded low by its finder.

## Trace: confirmed, low

The finding holds as described. With several public accounts, the pool page offers "Pay from my private balance anyway" whenever any account's public boxes sit in the pool. Both screens that confirm it say "your public account" and "that account" and never name an account: the question (Lovejoin.tsx:3649) and the review that follows (AgainReview, Lovejoin.tsx:3979). The worker's anyway path takes every box the wallet has in the pool, whichever account's mix put it there. Nothing checks the account in againBuild, againBoxes, fanOut or mixOutSubmit.

So on Account 2, with boxes from Account 1's public mix, Start mix ties the private UTxOs it spends to Account 1. "Your public account" reads as the account on screen. That is exactly the gap 1.6.0's x-privacy#0 fixed in the Bring one back questions, but this question was left out.

A wider case than the finder's: with Account 2's own boxes beside Account 1's, one private funding pays for both accounts' boxes' mixes. That ties both accounts to the private UTxOs, and so to each other, while the question says "that account", singular. privacy.md:180-182 promises that every screen that could make such a link says what it would reveal, and that co-spending names the accounts.

The finder left out one mitigation: the page names the other account just above the link. That is the OtherAccounts note at Lovejoin.tsx:1599 ("Some of your boxes came from a mix from Public account 1. Switch to it on the Public tab to mix them again."), shown whenever mixing again isn't refused. My test confirms it renders there. The user can also press "Keep them apart". The question and the review, where the user confirms, still name nobody.

Grade: low.
- **Who:** the user, by their own action.
- **Reach:** it needs several public accounts, another account's public mix with boxes left in the pool, and the anyway link pressed and confirmed.
- **Cost:** no money is lost. The cost is a privacy tie the user agreed to, but to an account the confirmation doesn't name. In the mixed case two public accounts get tied, a link the wallet elsewhere names (x-privacy#0) or refuses to make (1.2.0's C01 for Mix again from my public account).
- **Way out:** Keep them apart, plus the page note above the link. Once sent, the tie is on chain for good.

It isn't medium: the link is not made by default (it comes after a danger-styled confirm), and privacy.md:185 already says the anyway path "still takes every account's, after its warning". Only the warning's words are wrong. The finder also cites the key once as `lovejoin.privacy.privateAnyway.privacy.ties`; the code's key is `lovejoin.privateAnyway.privacy.ties`. Harmless.

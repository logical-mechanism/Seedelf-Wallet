# e1a#1: verification

From lane E1a, graded medium by its finder.

## Trace: confirmed, medium

The finding holds, and a run shows it. A stranger who reads a Seedelf name's register (two public points) can re-randomize it and deposit a 5 ₳ box under it, paid from their own key. The wallet's only ownership check is `b = [x]·a` with the Seedelf key, so it counts the box as its own. Its maker lookup records `{mixed:false}` with no `public` flag (no account key paid it). So the pool read holds the box as not mixed yet. Home then says "1 not mixed yet: open Lovejoin to see what to do", and the page says "One of your boxes isn't mixed yet … Mix my boxes again takes it first."

Mix my boxes again then:
- counts the box, since `privately` drops only public accounts' boxes;
- sorts it first;
- mixes `ours[..boxes]` in a chain whose fee and collateral come from a new one-time account, funded from the private balance.

In my test, with room for one box, the chain's only first-wave mix spent the stranger's box beside two others' boxes and the funding's output, and left the wallet's own box out. The stranger, watching that box, learns that the one-time account and its funding's private inputs and change belong to the name's owner, along with every box of that chain. privacy.md admits only the funding-to-account link ("one of the three … likely yours"), never a tie to the name, and no review warns.

Two corrections, neither material:
- fan_out shuffles the first wave, so the box goes into one of the first-wave mixes, not always the chain's first.
- The tie is an inference, not proof. Lovejoin's mixes need no owner's proof, so any mixer's random draw could take the box first. In a quiet pool the wallet's own Mix again is the likeliest spender, and two planted boxes taken into one chain make the inference near-certain.

Cost to the stranger: 5 ₳ a box, which the victim keeps, plus fees. The victim only has to press the button the wallet recommends.

Medium on the brief's scale: a privacy link the wallet makes by default, caused by a stranger. No funds are at risk, so not high. It isn't settled or known anywhere I searched. The owner's rules don't cover it either: the fix changes what a default selection takes, not a refusal of a valid choice.

## Impact: partly, low

The mechanism is real and I traced every step. Anyone who reads a Seedelf's register can deposit a 5 ₳ box (g·d, u·d) under it, since a deposit runs no script. The wallet then holds that box as its own not-mixed-yet deposit box, and Mix my boxes again takes it before any other box into a chain whose one-time account is funded from the private balance. The impact is narrower than the finding says, in four ways.

1. The planter gets an inference, not a tie. Lovejoin draws any free box into anyone's mix (fresh_for). So seeing the box spent in that session's first wave shows the session is the name owner's only as surely as the pool is quiet. In a busier pool another chain draws the box first. It then becomes an ordinary mixed box that comes back by itself and isn't taken first, and the planter's trail drops to one box in three.
2. The finding says every other box of the user's in that chain is tied to the name. Each one is only one of three inputs to its first mix, and the review already says "one box in each first mix is likely yours". The plant tells the planter whose boxes they are, not which ones.
3. The finding says the chain's first mix spends the planted box. Core shuffles each wave, so the box lands in one of the first wave's mixes, not always the first transaction.
4. It needs the user to press Mix my boxes again before any other chain draws the box. No money is lost: the user keeps the planter's 5 ₳.

What I learn about my own payment is what any payer learns: a 5 ₳ box is like a 5 ₳ payment the payer can follow. The project already notes this ("a payer knows theirs", review-1.7.1.md:60), and privacy.md's chain-reader model never claims to hide it.

The wallet's own part is still real. The history rules would keep a 5 ₳ Seedelf payment out of a session's funding. Mix again instead takes every box, this one first, and the callout recommends exactly that.

The strongest case for medium is privacy review §2.10: the same mechanism with the public account's deposit, graded Medium there and fixed by leaving those boxes out. That one needed no attacker and anyone reading the chain could see the link. I'm unsure between low and medium, so per the brief: low.

**Evidence:**

Paths are relative to TREE.

**1. Anyone can make the box.**
- PoolBox::from_row (seedelf-core/src/lovejoin/mod.rs:414-427) needs only three things:
  - the box value (value.rs:66-82);
  - the mix_box payment credential;
  - an inline Constr 0 [a, b] with a≠b, both in G1 (mod.rs:380-397).
- is_owned is `a * sk == b` (mod.rs:460-461), checked with the Seedelf key (wasm/src/lib.rs:6314 key.sk; wasm/src/lovejoin.rs:2605-2611).
- (g·d, u·d) from any Seedelf's on-chain register passes.

**2. The wallet holds it as its own deposit box.**
- madeBy (lovejoin.ts:6227-6265) gives a stranger-paid deposit {mixed:false}, with no mixedIn and no public flag.
- So mixedFor is false (1197-1198), and the box lands in depositsOf (2000-2007) and notMixedYet (2010).
- tests/lovejoin-restore.test.ts:749-768 pins a stranger-keyed deposit being mixed again "as any is".

**3. Mix my boxes again takes it first.**

**4. The link is probabilistic.**
- fresh_for (mod.rs:2509-2537) draws others uniformly from every free box that isn't the mixer's own, then shuffles.
- Any other wallet's chain therefore draws the planted box as an "other": 8 draws a box at the defaults, capped by the pool's free boxes.
- The planter can't tell "the session owns it" from "the session drew it", except by how quiet the pool is.
- Once another chain moves it, the box is mix-made (mixedIn). It comes back by itself and is no longer first, and the trail drops to one box in three.
- The planter can raise confidence with several plants, at 5 ₳ each.

**5. It needs the user's press.**

- A return's chain never takes ours: the non-again branch makes fresh registers and draws only from `pool` (wasm/src/lovejoin.rs:1655-1680).
- Left alone, the box stays held. Bring one back anyway ties only what a received payment ties.

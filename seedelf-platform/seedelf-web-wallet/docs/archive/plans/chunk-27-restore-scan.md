No server change.

## What it does

`SessionService.scan(network)` (`sessions.ts`), the `session-scan` request:

1. **Walks the one-time stake keys** (`2/i`) from 0, 20 at a time (`account_addresses`), until a batch has none used.
2. **For the used ones the book doesn't know:** what they hold (`credential_utxos`); each one's transactions (`account_txs` with `_after_block_height: 0`, one request an account); what those spent and paid (`tx_info`, inputs alone, whose outputs come with them).
3. **Their orders, through the swap that placed them:** each output the account's key-signed transactions paid to an order script `orders.json` pins, read by `readDexOrder` as the account's (`utxo_info`, `datum_info` for a datum kept by hash). Then `liveOrders` on the record decides which are open, a Splash fill's remnants too (since 1.5.0's release review, off the rows just read, asking `utxo_info` nothing more: C15).
4. **Adds a session found** (`recovered: { at, own }`) for each that holds something or has an order open: its funding (the oldest transaction its key didn't sign) as `out`, its swaps as `swap` with their orders, and what its key signed as `own`, which its return counts as the session's own. One holding nothing, with no order open, isn't added.
5. **Moves the book's `next`** past every used index. The merge is into the book as it is once everything's read, so a session started meanwhile keeps its index; one scan a network at a time.

It runs **after a restore** (in `handlers.ts`, behind the look for public accounts, on the network restored on) and from **Find leftovers**.

1.5.0's release review changed some of this: a server behind the one that showed an account used fails the scan rather than find nothing (C14); a scan is tied to the phrase it started on (C16); a restore marks every network until a scan finishes there, its own counting as under way from the restore on (C19); and a found session's funding keeps what the scan read at the account, so that one this device has sent nothing for ends by itself once nothing of it would come back (C02). [keys-and-accounts.md](../../keys-and-accounts.md#accounts) has each. (1.5.0's cross-area review: one whose record holds a swap ends only at a refresh 15 minutes or more after the first that found it so, since its order's fill pays the account in the very transaction that spends the order, X01; and a new session's funding no longer clears the restore's mark, X02.)

## Choices made here (the owner can overturn any)

- **A found session comes back by hand.** What it was for isn't known, so it has no `auto`, `swap`, `site` or `mix`; its page is the one sessions from before swaps ran themselves have (Swaps.tsx's `Session`), without Place the order: Cancel the order while one is open, Bring it back, or Bring everything back. Nothing runs by itself: the phrase may still be open in another browser whose swap or site uses the account, and its page says so (`swaps.warn.found`); so does Bring everything back's review since 1.5.0's release review (`claim.warn.found`, C18). (1.5.0's cross-area review: once it's over, its page says it's over in this browser, never that everything came back and the account is never used again, `swaps.next.closedFound`: X09.)
- **It's listed on the dApps page, under After a restore**, with Find leftovers beneath, not on Minswap's page: a found site's or mix's session isn't a swap. Its row is "Private session N", its page's aside *Found after a restore*, and Bring everything back names it the same way since 1.5.0's release review (C37). Bring everything back takes it when it holds money (not while its return's chain through Lovejoin is being sent, since that review: C09; a chain a lock or a closed browser cut before any of it went counts as stopped since 1.5.0's cross-area review, so it's taken: X03).
- **Find leftovers is on the dApps page**, where private sessions live, with its privacy note above the button: "It asks the server about your one-time accounts 20 at a time, so the server can tell they're one wallet's." (1.5.0's release review added the accounts the next sessions will use to it: C06.)
- **A restore scans the network it's made on, once.** The other network, or the phrase used in another browser since, needs the button. (Whether a restore should scan by itself at all is [the owner's question](../../post-release-roadmap.md#open-questions-for-the-owner) since 1.5.0's release review, C07.)
- **The restore's note on Home** adds "The restore also found 2 private sessions from before: bring them back on the dApps page." once the scan has written them (Home reads the sessions every 20 s). (1.5.0's cross-area review: it counts only those still to bring back, not one whose return is on its way: X11.)
- **Sessions the book knows are skipped, closed ones too.** A closed session whose account was paid again isn't reopened: its record's fields (`auto` above all) would start it running.
- **Remove wallet and Forgot password** now count "private sessions' money" among what the phrase brings back; *something no return takes is left in …* lost its "A restore won't show it yet."; a found session is named "(found after a restore)" in Remove wallet's list. (1.5.0's release review narrowed it to what their accounts hold, not what a site's session left open at the site: C17.)

## Cost

| | Requests |
|---|---|
| A phrase that never used a one-time account | 1 (`account_addresses`) |
| Each further batch of 20 indices | 1 |
| The used ones the book doesn't know | 1 `credential_utxos` per 75, 1 `account_txs` each, 1 `tx_info` per 20 of their transactions |
| Where those paid an order script | 1 `utxo_info` per 60 of those outputs (every account's together), 1 `datum_info` per 60 datums kept by hash, and 1 `utxo_info` per account holding nothing whose swaps placed orders (`liveOrders`), plus, for one whose spent order was Splash's, its remnant look. Since 1.5.0's release review `liveOrders` reads which are open off the first rows, so that second `utxo_info` is gone (C15, C35) |

A restore of a phrase with no sessions spends one request on it (the restore's e2e test counts it).

## Left for later

- **A found session whose account holds no ADA alone can't cancel its open order**: the wallet's own cancel needs one of the account's UTxOs as collateral. A top-up for found sessions would mend it ([Kept in mind](../../post-release-roadmap.md#kept-in-mind)).
- **What it can't find:** a session from before chunk 15b (the shared staking part: preprod, before any release), and an index past a run of 20 unused. (1.5.0's release review added what a site's session left open at the site, a listing or an order at a DEX `orders.json` doesn't pin: C17, in [keys-and-accounts.md](../../keys-and-accounts.md#accounts).)
- **A scan a lock or a worker stop cuts off** writes nothing; Find leftovers runs it again. (1.5.0's release review, C19: a restore marks every network until a scan finishes there, and the pages point to Find leftovers meanwhile; Kept in mind has it, ✅.)
